WHO WE ARE

Kigezi Tea Factory, also known as Kabale Tea Factory, is a tea processing facility located in Kabale District in Southwestern Uganda.

The factory was established to support tea farmers by providing a reliable market for fresh green tea leaves while promoting tea processing and value addition within the Kigezi region.

We process freshly harvested green tea leaves into high-quality black tea which is marketed locally and internationally. Through our operations, we contribute to employment, household income and the development of the local tea industry.

OUR STORY

Kigezi Tea Factory was established as part of efforts to promote tea growing, agro-processing and value addition in the Kigezi sub-region.

The Government of Uganda partnered with Kigezi Highland Tea Limited (KHTL), while the Uganda Development Corporation (UDC) supported the factory with processing equipment, generators and transport vehicles through a lease financing arrangement.

The factory was commissioned in August 2018 and introduced modern Cut, Tear and Curl (CTC) tea processing technology to the region.

OUR BACKGROUND

Kigezi Tea Factory is located in Kitumba Division along the Kabale-Katuna Road. The factory receives fresh green tea leaves mainly from out-grower farmers in Kabale and surrounding areas.

The factory's CTC processing line has a capacity of approximately 450 kg of green tea leaves per hour. The processed tea is converted into black tea for marketing and export.

By connecting farmers to a tea processing facility, the factory helps create a market for fresh tea leaves and contributes to the growth of tea production in Southwestern Uganda.
