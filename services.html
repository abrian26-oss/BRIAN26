OUR SERVICES

Quality tea services for farmers, customers and communities

Services Offered by Kigezi Tea Factory

Kigezi Tea Factory provides a range of services that support tea farmers and contribute to the development of the tea industry in the Kigezi region.

Our services include buying fresh green tea leaves, processing tea, quality management, tea marketing and supporting local tea-growing communities.

Buying Green Tea Leaves

We provide a reliable market for fresh green tea leaves produced by farmers in Kabale and surrounding areas. This helps farmers earn income from their tea production.

Tea Processing

We process freshly harvested green tea leaves into high-quality black tea using modern tea-processing equipment and appropriate processing methods.

Tea Quality Management

The factory focuses on maintaining the quality of tea during processing to ensure that the finished product meets the required standards.

Tea Marketing

Processed tea is marketed to buyers and tea markets. The factory helps connect locally produced tea with wider markets.

Tea Export

We support the marketing of processed tea to external markets, contributing to Uganda's tea export industry and foreign exchange earnings.

Farmer Support

The factory supports tea-growing communities by providing a dependable market for their produce and contributing to improved household incomes.

Our Services to Tea Farmers

Tea farmers are important partners in the success of Kigezi Tea Factory. The factory provides services that help farmers participate in the tea value chain.

  • Providing a market for freshly harvested green tea leaves.
  • Supporting tea production in Kabale and surrounding areas.
  • Promoting value addition through tea processing.
  • Creating opportunities for farmers to earn income from tea.
  • Supporting the growth of the local tea industry.
