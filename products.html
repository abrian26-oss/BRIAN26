Our Main Product: Black Tea

Kigezi Tea Factory specializes in processing green tea leaves into black tea. The processing operation adds value to tea grown by farmers in the Kigezi region.

Tea Processing

Fresh green tea leaves are received from farmers and processed using tea-processing equipment. The processed tea is then prepared for marketing and sale.

Market

Processed tea can be marketed through established tea markets, including the Mombasa Tea Auction, where tea from East Africa is traded.

Product Quality

Kigezi Tea Factory aims to maintain high standards in tea processing and handling. Quality is important at every stage of the tea value chain.

  • Fresh green tea leaves are collected from tea farmers.
  • Tea leaves are processed using appropriate processing methods and equipment.
  • Processed tea is handled carefully before marketing.
  • The factory focuses on producing tea suitable for established tea markets.
  • Value addition helps increase the economic benefits of tea production in the Kigezi region.

Supporting the Tea Value Chain

Our products represent an important part of the tea value chain in Southwestern Uganda. By purchasing green tea leaves from farmers and processing them into finished tea, the factory contributes to value addition, employment and household incomes.
