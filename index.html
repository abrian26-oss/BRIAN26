BACKGROUND

Kabale Tea factory also known as Kigezi tea factory is located in Kabale district in Southwestern Uganda. The factory was established as part of the government of Uganda's strategy to promote tea growing, agro-processing, value addition employment and household incomes, in the kigezi sub-region. The government partnered with the Kigezi Highland Tea Limited (KHTL) while the Uganda Development Corporation (UDC) provided processing equipment, generators and transport vehicles through a Lease Financing arrangement. The factory was commissioned in August 2018 and has a cut, tear and curl (CTC) processing line with a capacity of around 450kgs of green tea leaves per hour. It processes green tea leaves supplied mainly br the out-grower farmers from Kabale and surrounding areas into black tea.

MISSION: To process and produce high-quality tea while providing market for local area farmers and improving the livelihoods of communities in the Kigezi region.

VISION: To become a leading tea-processing company in Uganda, recognized for quality tea products, sustainable tea production, value addition, and improved livelihoods for tea growing communities.
