Kabale Tea Factory

Kabale Tea Factory is a tea processing facility located in Kabale District, Southwestern Uganda. It was established to support tea farmers by providing a reliable market for their fresh tea leaves and to promote value addition in the region.

The factory processes green tea leaves into high-quality black tea which is mainly marketed through the Mombasa Tea Auction.

It also contributes to employment, household income and the development of the local tea industry.

BACKGROUND

Kabale Tea Factory, also known as Kigezi Tea Factory, is located in Kabale District in Southwestern Uganda. The factory was established as part of the Government of Uganda's strategy to promote tea growing, agro-processing, value addition, employment and household incomes in the Kigezi sub-region.

The government partnered with Kigezi Highland Tea Limited (KHTL), while the Uganda Development Corporation (UDC) provided processing equipment, generators and transport vehicles through a Lease Financing arrangement.

The factory was commissioned in August 2018 and has a Cut, Tear and Curl (CTC) processing line with a capacity of around 450 kg of green tea leaves per hour.

It processes green tea leaves supplied mainly by out-grower farmers from Kabale and surrounding areas into black tea.

MISSION

To process and produce high-quality tea while providing market for local area farmers and improving the livelihoods of communities in the Kigezi region.

VISION

To become a leading tea-processing company in Uganda, recognized for quality tea products, sustainable tea production, value addition, and improved livelihoods for tea growing communities.
